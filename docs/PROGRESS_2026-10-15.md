# Session-relative planner map heights

The fresh aircraft session `20261014_233804_742867` completed localization, visual forwarding and parameter checks, then the ground auditor stopped with zero accepted references because no committed trajectory arrived within eight seconds. Concurrent 30-second native vision sampling remained fresh: maximum header age 60 ms and receive gap 41 ms. The planner reported `Odom below virtual ground` and invalid-corridor optimization; the polynomial topic had zero publishers. This was a planner failure, not a completed audit.

The current YAML configured virtual ground/ceiling at -1.5/3.5 m, initial map centre Z at 1.5 m, inflation resolution 0.2 m and inflation step 2. Pinned SUPER/ROG-Map uses `ORIGIN_AT_CORNER`; its grid/inflation adjustment makes the effective virtual floor -1.1 m. The observed raw odometry Z was -1.182715709 m, below that floor, even though the separate aligned/FC session origin was near zero.

The managed `low-speed-3d` planner now creates a separate session YAML before launch. A read-only helper captures three seconds of fresh stationary raw odometry while connected, disarmed and grounded, then freezes a grid-aligned vertical offset for that core session. Only virtual ground, virtual ceiling and initial map-centre Z shift. At the recorded height, the shift is -1.2 m: configured ground/ceiling become -2.7/2.3 m and map centre Z becomes 0.3 m; effective bounds become -2.3/1.7 m. The vertical span is preserved. Source YAML, point clouds, robot radius, velocity/acceleration, goal coordinates and physical 10 cm flight/reference margins are unchanged.

The reference record is retained in the current session log directory. Repeated planner starts reuse it instead of accumulating offsets or following drift. A changed raw publisher/alignment or raw ground-height change over 0.10 m refuses reuse. A new core session gets its own record. Creating a session profile is not physical floor calibration, map relocalization or a localization-drift repair.

Validation passed four pure configuration/immutability cases, 36 manager lifecycle cases and three deployment-layout cases. Five isolated DDS capture cases covered healthy input, movement, raw dropout, arming and duplicate publishers. The healthy case also ran the real helper twice as subprocesses, confirming byte-identical profile/reference reuse and parent telemetry refresh. Seven translated-height calculations matched compiled expressions extracted from pinned upstream ROG-Map's boundary calculation. This cross-check validates the arithmetic, not full trajectory optimization. Aircraft confirmation of the new map profile and the complete ground audit remains pending.
